Honors Program

The Minnesota State Mankato Honors Program develops students in three competencies: leadership; research, scholarly and creative activity; and intercultural engagement.

Why Honors

Honors students complete eight experiences across these competencies, at a development level (building the skill) and an application level (putting it to use), and reflect on each one. These essays explain why I joined the program and what it has meant to me.
