Honors Program

The Minnesota State Mankato Honors Program develops students in three competencies: leadership, research, scholarly and creative activity, and intercultural engagement.

Honors students complete eight experiences across these competencies, at both a development level (building the skill) and an application level (putting it to use), and reflect on each one. This portfolio is where those experiences and reflections live, and it's what I'll defend at the end of the program.
